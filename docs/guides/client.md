# Guide for the client

You commissioned the due diligence. On the platform you follow progress, read the findings the partner has
released, ask questions and download the issued reports. Read [Getting started](README.md#getting-started-everyone)
first: you sign in with a password and an authenticator app, and accept the NDA at your first sign-in.

You do not see the data room or the request list. The consultants work with the target's documents and report to
you through findings and reports.

## 1. Progress

Open the project. The **Dashboard** shows:

- how much of the request list the target has fulfilled;
- items due soon and overdue;
- the number of findings by risk level.

## 2. Findings

**Findings** lists the findings the partner has **released** to you. Drafts and findings under review stay internal.

Each finding shows:

- its number (for example `F-LEG-001`), workstream and category;
- the description, impact and recommendation;
- the risk rating: impact × likelihood, each from 1 to 5, giving **Low**, **Medium**, **High** or **Critical**.

The heat map shows how the released findings spread over impact and likelihood. You are notified when a new finding
is released.

## 3. Questions (Q&A)

1. **Q&A → Ask a question.** Write your question and click **Send**. The team decides the workstream, recipient
   and due date.
2. The project manager or partner reviews your question before it goes to the target. It shows as **Awaiting
   approval**. They may return it to you with feedback.
3. You can read the answer once the team has reviewed it and **closed** the question. You are notified then.

You see your own questions, not those of the consulting team.

## 4. Reports

**Reports** lists the versions the partner has **issued** to you. You are notified when a new one is issued.

**PDF** downloads the report, watermarked with your name, e-mail, IP address and the time. Every download is
recorded.

The report contains:

- a cover with the project and target company;
- an executive summary;
- the risk heat map;
- the findings per workstream, with their source documents.

## Tips

- Forwarding reports outside your organization is subject to the engagement letter. The watermark identifies the copy.
- For questions about a finding, use Q&A or contact the engagement partner directly.
