# Guide for the target company

You represent the company under review. The consulting team asks for documents and information through the
platform. You deliver them here, and answer their questions. Read [Getting started](README.md#getting-started-everyone)
first: you sign in with a password and an authenticator app, and accept the NDA at your first sign-in.

What you see:

- the **Request list**;
- the **Data room** folders shared with you;
- **Q&A** questions sent to you.

You never see the consultants' findings, internal notes or reports.

## 1. The request list

**Request list → open the project.** Items appear in waves, as the team releases them. You get an e-mail and a
notification for each new wave.

Each item shows:

- the number, for example `LEG-001`;
- what is requested;
- the priority and due date;
- the person in charge on your side (or "Whole target team");
- its status: **Requested**, **Partially received**, **Received**, **Rejected** or **Closed**.

Use the filters for status, workstream and wave, or the search box. Reminders arrive by e-mail for items due soon or
overdue, at most once a day.

## 2. Delivering documents

1. Click the item. Under **Attach a document**:
   - optionally write a **Note for the reviewers**;
   - choose the **Data-room folder**;
   - then either **Upload and attach** a file from your computer, or choose a document already in that folder and
     click **Attach**.

   You can only upload into folders where you have upload permission. If none is shared with you, ask the project
   manager.
2. Attach everything the item asks for, then report it:
   - **Partially delivered**: something is still missing;
   - **Completely delivered**: everything is there.

   You need at least one attached document first. The team is notified.
3. The team then **closes** the item or **rejects** the delivery with a reason. A rejected item is open again: read
   the reason, add what is missing and report it again.

Every file is scanned for malware and checked before anyone can open it. While that runs it shows as **Scanning**.

Comments under an item go to the team. Use them for clarifications that are not documents.

## 3. The data room

The **Data room** shows the folders shared with you.

- In folders where you may upload, drag files in or click to choose them. Turn on **Upload folder** to drop a whole
  folder; its subfolders are created too.
- Uploading a file with the same name again adds a new version instead of a duplicate.
- Opening a document shows its pages in the browser, watermarked with your name, e-mail, IP address and the time.
- Downloading and printing are only possible where the team allows them.

## 4. Questions (Q&A)

The team sends you questions through **Q&A** (after internal approval), and you are notified.

1. Open the question and write your answer.
2. Attach supporting documents in the same panel. Choose the folder, then **Upload and attach** a new file or pick an
   existing document. Uploaded files are also stored in the data room.
3. **Send answer.** You can update the answer while the question is open.

The team may close the question or ask a follow-up. A follow-up comes back to you with their message.

## Tips

- Use clear file names (for example `2024 Audited Financial Statements.pdf`). They are what the team searches for.
- Upload originals (PDF, Word, Excel), not photos of screens. Scans are fine: the platform reads their text.
- Executable files and similar types are not accepted.
- If something is confidential beyond the data room's normal rules, tell the project manager before uploading it.
