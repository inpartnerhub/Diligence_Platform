# User guides

Task-oriented guides for every role on the Diligence Platform, used for training (see the
[training plan](training-plan.md)) and as reference afterwards.

| Guide | For |
|-------|-----|
| [Consultant team](consultant-team.md) | Partners, project managers, analysts and observers of the consulting firm |
| [Target company](target.md) | Representatives of the company being reviewed (sell side) |
| [Client](client.md) | Representatives of the buyer or investor (buy side) |
| [Administrator](administrator.md) | IT administrators (super admin) |

Screens are in Indonesian by default; switch to English with the language selector in the header. These guides use
the English labels.

## Getting started (everyone)

### Signing in

1. Open the platform address you received. You are taken to the sign-in page.
2. Enter your e-mail (or username) and password.
   - Internal users of the consulting firm may see a button to sign in with the company account (Microsoft or
     Google) instead.
3. **Two-factor authentication.** Target, client, observer and administrator accounts must use an authenticator app
   (Microsoft Authenticator, Google Authenticator or FreeOTP).
   - The first time, scan the QR code and enter the 6-digit code.
   - Afterwards, enter the current code at every sign-in.
4. **NDA.** External users accept the confidentiality terms (NDA) at their first sign-in.

Your session ends after 30 minutes without activity. When you sign in again you return to the page you were on.
After five wrong passwords your account is locked for a short time.

### Your profile

Open **My profile** from the menu under your name. It shows:

- your roles;
- whether two-factor authentication is on;
- your last login;
- the **Interface language**.

**Manage password and authenticator** opens the account page, where you change your password or register a new
phone.

### Notifications

The bell in the header shows unread notifications: new requests, deliveries, findings to review, questions,
reports, reminders. **View all notifications** opens the full list.

Under **Delivery preferences** you choose, per notification type, whether it also arrives by **e-mail** or only
**in app**.

### Confidentiality built in

- Every page you view and every file you download carries your name, e-mail, IP address and the time as a
  watermark.
- Every view and download is recorded in the audit log.
- Each project is separate. You only see projects you are a member of, and only the parts your role allows.

## Statuses at a glance

| Area | Statuses |
|------|----------|
| Request items | Not requested → Requested → Partially received / Received → Closed; Rejected sends the item back to the target |
| Documents | Scanning → Clean (viewable); Malware, Type not allowed and Scan failed are never shown |
| Findings | Draft → In review → Approved → Released; Archived (partners only) |
| Questions | Draft → Awaiting approval → Sent → Answered → Closed |
| Reports | Draft → Issued |
